Why are post-grad expenses higher than university?
  • Council Tax: Full-time students pay £0 Council Tax. Graduates must pay full Council Tax (£80–£140+/month).
  • Upgraded Housing: Higher standards for professional flatshares vs standard student HMOs.
  • Work & Lifestyle Expenses: Daily commuting, professional wardrobe/workwear, gym memberships, and a more active professional social life.
